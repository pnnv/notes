POWERGRID’s IT support is fragmented across GLPI, Solman, and email, causing slow responses, lost tickets, and low employee satisfaction. A unified, AI-driven helpdesk is needed to consolidate inputs, automate triage, and offer 24/7 self-service. We propose a single-page application (React + Node.js + MongoDB) with Google Gemini as the central intelligence: it ingests messages, classifies and prioritizes tickets, routes them to the right teams, and powers a conversational self-service bot. Benefits include faster resolution, reduced L1 workload, lower operational costs, actionable analytics, and higher employee satisfaction, delivering measurable productivity and scalable IT operations.
